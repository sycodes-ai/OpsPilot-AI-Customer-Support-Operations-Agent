# Warranty

## Warranty period
Tents carry a 2-year warranty. Jackets, backpacks, sleeping bags and footwear carry a 1-year warranty. Accessories carry a 6-month warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as broken zips, failed seams, delaminated waterproof coating and faulty buckles.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, misuse, alterations and damage from improper washing are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number, the email used for the order and clear photos of the defect. A support agent reviews each claim within 3 business days and offers a repair, a replacement or a refund.
