# Shipping and Delivery

## Domestic shipping in Korea
How long does delivery take? Standard delivery takes 2 to 3 business days. Standard shipping is free on orders over 50,000 KRW; otherwise it costs 3,000 KRW.

## Express delivery
Express delivery arrives the next business day if you order before 2 pm KST. Express costs 6,000 KRW. Express is not available on weekends or public holidays.

## International shipping
We ship to Japan, Singapore, the United States and Germany. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer.

## Tracking
When your order ships, we send an email with the carrier name and tracking number. Domestic orders ship with SwiftPost and international orders with GlobalWay. You can also ask the support assistant for your order status with your order number and email.

## Delayed or lost parcels
If a parcel has not moved for 5 business days, contact support. We open an investigation with the carrier and send a replacement or a full refund if the parcel is confirmed lost.
