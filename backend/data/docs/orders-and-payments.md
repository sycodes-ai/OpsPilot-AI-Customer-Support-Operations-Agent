# Orders, Cancellations and Payments

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order free of charge while its status is "processing", before it ships. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; please use the return process after delivery instead.

## Changing an order
The delivery address or items can be changed only while the order is still processing. Contact support with your order number and the email used for the order.

## Order status meanings
- Processing: we received the order and are packing it.
- Shipped: the parcel is with the carrier and has a tracking number.
- Delivered: the carrier confirmed delivery.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and any payment was refunded.
- Returned: the return was received and refunded.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards, mobile payments and bank transfer. Payment is taken when the order is placed. We never ask for your card number, password or one-time codes in chat or email.

## Price changes and discounts
Prices and discounts cannot be changed after an order is placed, and the support assistant cannot create discount codes. Promotional codes must be entered at checkout.
