# Privacy and Account Security

## Verifying who you are
To protect your information, order details are shared only after you provide both the order number and the email address used for that order. The assistant never shows another customer's details.

## What we will never ask for
We never ask for your password, full card number, CVC or one-time codes. If someone asks for these in our name, do not share them and contact support.

## Personal data
We use your personal data only to process orders, deliveries, returns and support requests. You can ask a human agent to export or delete your data.
