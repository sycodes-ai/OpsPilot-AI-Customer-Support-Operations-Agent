# Frequently Asked Questions

## When is customer support available?
Human support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 KST. The support assistant answers questions 24 hours a day and hands over to a human when needed.

## How do I contact a human agent?
Ask the assistant to talk to a person, or email support@nimbus-outfitters.example. The assistant creates a ticket and a team member replies within one business day.

## How do I find my size?
Each product page has a size chart. Our footwear runs true to size; if you are between sizes, choose the larger size for hiking boots.

## How should I wash my rain jacket?
Wash waterproof jackets on a gentle cycle at 30°C with technical wash, without fabric softener. Tumble dry on low heat to restore the water-repellent finish.

## Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Digital gift cards from 10,000 to 500,000 KRW are valid for 5 years and cannot be returned or exchanged for cash.

## Do you have a student discount?
Students get 10% off their first order after verifying their student status on the website.

## Do you price match?
We do not offer price matching.
