# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
How long do I have to return an item? You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the date the order was delivered, not the date it was ordered. Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging with tags attached.

## How to start a return
Start a return in the chat or on the website with your order number and the email address used for the order. Once the return is approved you receive a return code (RMA code) and a prepaid label by email. Pack the item, attach the label and drop it at any SwiftPost point within 7 days.

## Return shipping cost
Returns for a change of mind cost 5,000 KRW, which is deducted from the refund. Returns of defective, damaged or wrong items are free.

## Refund timing
When will I get my refund? Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection. Card statements can take another 2 to 3 business days to show the refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Items marked Final Sale, opened hygiene products and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery, once per order. Exchanges are free if the new item is in stock; otherwise we refund the original item.

## Large refunds
Refunds above 500,000 KRW are reviewed by a member of the customer support team before they are approved.
