# Product Guide

## Trailblazer Rain Jacket (NB-JKT-01)
A lightweight waterproof shell with taped seams, an adjustable hood and pit zips. Waterproof rating 10,000 mm. Weight 320 g.

## Summit Down Jacket (NB-JKT-02)
A warm 650-fill down jacket for cold, dry conditions. Packs into its own pocket. Not designed for heavy rain.

## Ridge 30L Daypack (NB-BAG-01)
A 30-litre daypack with a hydration sleeve, rain cover and padded hip belt. Fits laptops up to 15 inches.

## Voyager 60L Backpack (NB-BAG-02)
A 60-litre trekking pack with an adjustable back system, lid pocket and side access zip.

## Alpine 2-Person Tent (NB-TNT-01)
A freestanding 3-season tent for two people. Weight 2.1 kg. Flysheet waterproof rating 3,000 mm. Two doors and two porches.

## Basecamp 4-Person Tent (NB-TNT-02)
A family tent for four people with a large vestibule and standing height of 1.9 m. Weight 6.4 kg.

## Trail Runner Shoes (NB-SHO-01)
Lightweight trail running shoes with a grippy lugged sole and a breathable mesh upper.

## Hiking Boots Pro (NB-SHO-02)
Waterproof leather hiking boots with ankle support and a stiff sole for rocky trails.

## Cloudrest Sleeping Bag (NB-SLP-01)
A synthetic sleeping bag with a comfort temperature of 2°C and a compression sack.

## Compact Camping Stove (NB-STV-01)
A small gas stove that boils one litre of water in about 4 minutes. Uses standard screw-on gas canisters, sold separately.

## Accessories
Headlamp 300 (NB-LMP-01) gives 300 lumens and is USB rechargeable. Insulated Water Bottle 750ml (NB-BTL-01) keeps drinks cold for 24 hours. Thermal Gloves (NB-GLV-01), Sun Trek Hat (NB-HAT-01) and Trekking Poles (NB-PLE-01) complete the range.
